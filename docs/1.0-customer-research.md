# BookMyTurf — Customer Research (1.0)

## Purpose
Validate product assumptions with real turf owners before building. These questions help understand their current workflow, pain points, and willingness to pay.

---

## Section 1: Business Overview

### Q1: Tell me about your turf business — how many courts, what sports, how long have you been running?

**Sample Response:**
> "I have 2 turfs in Andheri and Powai. Andheri has 3 courts — 2 for cricket and 1 for football. Powai is smaller, just 1 cricket court and 1 badminton. Running for 2 years now."

**Counter Questions:**
- How many bookings do you get per day on average?
- Which sport brings the most revenue?
- Do you plan to open more locations?

---

### Q2: How do you currently manage bookings?

**Sample Response:**
> "Mostly through WhatsApp and phone calls. We have a register at the counter. Sometimes my staff writes it on a board. We also have a few regulars who just show up."

**Counter Questions:**
- How many bookings are missed because of phone/WhatsApp confusion?
- Do you ever face double-booking situations?
- How much time per day do you or your staff spend on managing bookings?

---

### Q3: What's your biggest headache in running this business day-to-day?

**Sample Response:**
> "Double bookings happen at least 2-3 times a week. Customers fight. Also, I can't track revenue properly — my staff sometimes doesn't record cash payments. And on weekends, phone is ringing non-stop, I can't attend all calls."

**Counter Questions:**
- What do you do when a double-booking happens? Who handles it?
- How much revenue do you think you lose monthly due to these issues?
- Have you tried any app or software to solve this?

---

## Section 2: Booking & Scheduling

### Q4: What are your operating hours and how do you define time slots?

**Sample Response:**
> "We open at 6 AM, close at 11 PM. Cricket slots are 1 hour, badminton is 30 minutes. On weekends we sometimes do 2-hour tournament blocks."

**Counter Questions:**
- Do you charge differently for morning vs evening (peak hours)?
- What are your peak hours?
- Do customers ever want to book for longer durations (2-3 hours)?

---

### Q5: How do you handle pricing? Is it the same all day?

**Sample Response:**
> "No, evenings are expensive. 6 PM to 10 PM is ₹1200/hour for cricket. Morning 6 AM to 10 AM is ₹800. Weekends are ₹1500 flat. Badminton is ₹400 off-peak, ₹600 peak."

**Counter Questions:**
- Do you offer any discounts for bulk bookings (e.g., 10 sessions)?
- Do you give different prices for regular/loyal customers?
- Do you change prices during festivals or summers?

---

### Q6: How far in advance do customers typically book?

**Sample Response:**
> "Most book same day or 1 day before. Some corporates book 1 week ahead for team events. Tournaments need 2-3 weeks advance booking."

**Counter Questions:**
- Would you want to limit how far in advance someone can book?
- Do you take advance payment for future bookings?
- Do no-shows happen? How often?

---

## Section 3: Payments & Cancellation

### Q7: How do customers pay you currently?

**Sample Response:**
> "Mostly UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe) at the counter. Some pay cash. Corporate clients do bank transfer. Nobody pays online in advance — they pay when they come."

**Counter Questions:**
- Would you be open to customers paying online at the time of booking?
- Do you face issues with customers booking and not showing up?
- If they paid in advance, do you think no-shows would reduce?

---

### Q8: What happens when a customer cancels?

**Sample Response:**
> "If they call 3-4 hours before, we adjust. If they cancel last minute or don't show up, we lose that slot — can't fill it that late. Very frustrating on weekends."

**Counter Questions:**
- Do you currently charge any cancellation fee?
- Would you want a system that automatically handles refunds based on your rules?
- What refund policy would you set? (e.g., full refund if 12hrs before, no refund if <3hrs)

---

### Q9: How do you track revenue today?

**Sample Response:**
> "I check my UPI transaction history and ask my staff. End of month, we roughly calculate. I don't have exact data on which court or which sport earns more."

**Counter Questions:**
- Would a dashboard showing daily/weekly/monthly revenue be useful?
- Would knowing your peak hours help you adjust pricing or staffing?
- Do you file GST? Do you need invoice generation?

## Section 4: Staff & Operations

### Q10: How many staff do you have and what do they do?

**Sample Response:**
> "I have 3 people. One manager handles the phone and WhatsApp bookings. Two boys at the ground — they set up nets, give equipment, handle the lights. I visit once a day to check."

**Counter Questions:**
- Does your manager have authority to give discounts or block slots?
- Do you trust your staff with cash handling?
- Would it help if staff could mark attendance/check-in via an app?

---

### Q11: Do you face issues with staff managing bookings without you?

**Sample Response:**
> "Yes. My manager sometimes books for his friends at lower rates. I can't track what really happened when I wasn't there. Also if he's busy, calls go unanswered and we lose customers."

**Counter Questions:**
- Would role-based access help? (Manager can create bookings but can't change pricing)
- Would you want to get real-time notifications when a booking is made?
- Would a log of all changes (who did what, when) be useful?

## Section 6: Technology & Willingness to Pay

### Q14: Do you or your staff use any apps for business currently?

**Sample Response:**
> "Just WhatsApp and Google Pay. I tried Playo once but they take too much commission and send random customers — I can't control quality. I wanted my own system."

**Counter Questions:**
- What didn't you like about Playo/Hudle specifically?
- Would you prefer a system you control (your own booking page) vs being listed on a marketplace?
- What's the one feature you'd want most?

---

### Q15: If a system handled all bookings, payments, and staff tracking — what would you pay monthly?

**Sample Response:**
> "If it actually saves me time and prevents double-bookings... maybe ₹1000-2000 per month. Not more unless it's really bringing me new customers. I don't want high commission like Playo (15-20%)."

**Counter Questions:**
- What if commission was only 5% (not 15-20%) and only on online bookings?
- Would a 14-day free trial convince you to try it?
- Would you pay more for additional features like analytics, WhatsApp notifications, or multiple location support?

---

### Q16: What would make you NOT use a booking system?

**Sample Response:**
> "If it's too complicated for my staff. They're not very tech-savvy. Also if customers find it confusing and still call me, then what's the point. And if it's too expensive."

**Counter Questions:**
- What if your staff only needed to tap a button to confirm a walk-in?
- What if customers could book via a simple WhatsApp shared link?
- Would a 30-minute training session for your team be acceptable?

## Section 9: Maintenance & Ground Operations

### Q21: How do you handle turf maintenance — watering, repairs, resurfacing?

**Sample Response:**
> "We water the turf twice a day — morning 5 AM and after last slot at 11 PM. Resurfacing happens once a year. Small repairs we do every month. During maintenance, we block that court for a day."

**Counter Questions:**
- How do you currently inform customers when a court is under maintenance?
- Do customers get frustrated when they arrive and court is blocked?
- Would an automatic "court unavailable" notice on the booking page help?

---

### Q22: Do you provide equipment (balls, bats, shoes, bibs)?

**Sample Response:**

**Counter Questions:**
- Do you track equipment inventory?
- Is equipment rental a significant revenue source?
- Would you want to add equipment booking alongside slot booking? (e.g., "Add shoes +₹50")

**Counter Questions:**

- Do power cuts cause cancellations? How often?

## Interview Tips

1. **Don't sell** — listen. You're validating, not pitching.
2. **Record the call** (with permission) — you'll miss details otherwise.
3. **Talk to 5-10 owners** before building — patterns emerge after 5 interviews.
4. **Ask "why" 3 times** — first answer is surface-level, real pain comes deeper.
5. **Note their exact words** — use their language in your marketing later.
6. **Watch for energy shifts** — when they get animated/frustrated, you've hit a real pain point.
