# BookMyTurf — Competitor Analysis (1.1)

## Overview
Analysis of existing sports turf/venue booking platforms in India. Identifies their model, strengths, weaknesses, and our differentiation.

---

## Market Landscape

The Indian sports venue booking market has two categories:

| Category | Model | Examples |
|----------|-------|---------|
| **Marketplace** (B2C) | Platform lists many venues; customers discover & book; platform takes commission | Playo, Hudle, KheloMore, District |
| **SaaS** (B2B) | Platform provides tools to venue owners; owners manage their own bookings | TurfSys, TurfBooking.in, **BookMyTurf (us)** |

---

## Key Competitors

### 1. Playo (Marketplace)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Model** | Marketplace — customers discover and book venues via Playo app |
| **Funding** | Well-funded, 411+ venues in Bangalore alone |
| **Sports** | 50+ sports including cricket, football, badminton, pickleball |
| **For Venue Owners** | Partner app, booking management, visibility on Playo platform |
| **Commission** | 15-20% per booking (reported by turf owners) |
| **Strengths** | Large customer base, brand recognition, insurance feature, app quality |
| **Weaknesses** | High commission, venue owners lose control, random customer quality, venue can't build own brand |
| **Pricing for Venue** | Free to list, but high commission on every booking |

### 2. Hudle (Marketplace)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Model** | Marketplace — venue discovery + booking + community |
| **Funding** | $2.5M Series A (2025), Sky Impact Capital |
| **Reach** | 2,000+ venues across 60 Indian cities |
| **Sports** | Badminton, pickleball, padel, football, cricket |
| **For Venue Owners** | Partner app for booking management and business insights |
| **Strengths** | Growing fast, emerging sports focus (pickleball, padel), community features |
| **Weaknesses** | Newer brand, smaller user base than Playo, commission model same issue |
| **Pricing for Venue** | Commission-based |

### 3. KheloMore (Marketplace + SaaS hybrid)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Model** | Venue partner app — manages bookings, no complex dashboards |
| **Sports** | Cricket, football, badminton, pickleball |
| **For Venue Owners** | Simplified partner app, booking management |
| **Strengths** | Simple UI, mobile-first for venue owners |
| **Weaknesses** | Smaller market presence, limited analytics |

### 4. District by Eternal (Marketplace — new entrant)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Model** | Marketplace by Eternal (formerly Zomato group) |
| **Launch** | Late 2024 |
| **Sports** | Badminton, pickleball, football, basketball, swimming, box cricket |
| **Strengths** | Backed by Eternal (deep pockets, tech talent), aggressive expansion |
| **Weaknesses** | New, unproven, scouting acquisitions (talked to Hudle) |

### 5. TurfSys (SaaS — Direct Competitor)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Model** | SaaS — management tool for turf owners (NOT marketplace) |
| **Features** | Click-grid time slots, dynamic pricing, AI chat assistant, Hindi + English |
| **Setup** | 5-minute setup, no credit card needed |
| **Strengths** | India-focused SaaS, Hindi support, fast setup |
| **Weaknesses** | New/small, unclear pricing, limited brand recognition |
| **Pricing** | Free to start (details unclear) |

### 6. TurfBooking.in (Marketplace — no commission)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Model** | Marketplace — lists venues at exact venue price, no markup |
| **Reach** | 80+ venues in Indore, Mumbai, Kolkata, Delhi, others |
| **Differentiator** | "No hidden charges. No commission." |
| **For Venue Owners** | Free listing, more visibility |
| **Strengths** | No commission = attractive to owners, community powered |
| **Weaknesses** | No revenue model clarity, limited features for owners |

| Differentiator | vs Marketplaces (Playo/Hudle) | vs SaaS (TurfSys) |
|---------------|------------------------------|-------------------|
| **Owner controls everything** | Playo owns the customer relationship. We give it back to the owner. | Same advantage |
| **Low commission (3-8%)** | vs 15-20% on Playo/Hudle | We have subscription + commission hybrid for predictable revenue |
| **Walk-in + Online combined** | Marketplaces only track online. We track 100% of bookings. | TurfSys also does this — we're comparable |

| **WhatsApp notifications** | Not offered for venue owners | TurfSys unclear |

## Key Insight for Sales

When pitching to turf owners, our message is:

> **"Playo/Hudle take 15-20% of your revenue and own your customers. BookMyTurf gives you your own booking page, charges only 3-8%, and tracks ALL your bookings (online + walk-in). Your brand, your customers, your data."**

## Market Opportunity

- India has 10,000+ sports turfs (growing 30% YoY)
- 90% use WhatsApp/phone for bookings (undigitized)
- Marketplace model alienates owners (high commission, loss of control)
- SaaS model is underserved — TurfSys is the only direct competitor and is early stage
- Our total addressable market: 10,000 venues × ₹2,000/month avg = ₹20 Cr/year opportunity
